## 🎨 TONE & VOICE GUIDELINES

### Transform From:
- SaaS marketing copy
- Product-centric language
- "Enterprise" focused
- Fast-paced startup tone

### Transform To:
- Academic excellence focused
- Institution-centric language
- Tradition meets innovation
- Prestigious, modern, trustworthy tone
- Examples:
  - "Beautiful dashboards" → "Institutional intelligence"
  - "Your team" → "Your institution/campus"
  - "Customers" → "Partner institutions/stakeholders"
  - "Startup features" → "Academic capabilities"
  - "SaaS solution" → "Integrated ERP Ecosystem"

## 🎯 KEY PRINCIPLES

1. **Keep UI Intact** - No design changes, only content
2. **Maintain Modern Feel** - Premium, contemporary, tech-forward
3. **Authentic University Voice** - Not corporate, not startup
4. **Inclusive Language** - Appeal to all campus stakeholders
5. **Data-Driven** - Use real statistics and metrics
6. **Forward-Looking** - Focus on future of education
7. **Accessible** - Professional yet approachable
8. **Results-Oriented** - Show value and impact
